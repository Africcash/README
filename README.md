# AfricCash

### Bringing local cash access to Stellar.

**AfricCash** is a peer-to-peer cash-out platform that connects **USDC holders on Stellar** with trusted local cash agents across African markets.

The idea is simple: bring the familiar **POS and mobile money agent model** into the Stellar ecosystem, giving users a convenient way to discover nearby agents and exchange digital assets for physical cash.


## The Problem

Across Nigeria and other African markets, POS and mobile money agents have become an important way for people to access physical cash.

But this familiar model doesn't currently extend effectively to people holding **USDC on Stellar**.

Users may have digital funds but lack a convenient local way to convert those funds into cash, particularly in areas where **Stellar Anchors or other off-ramp infrastructure** are unavailable.

**AfricCash bridges that gap.**



##  How AfricCash Works

### 1. Connect Your Wallet

Users connect a **non-custodial Stellar wallet** to AfricCash.

### 2. Find a Cash Agent

Discover nearby agents through an interactive map.

Users can view relevant agent information before starting a transaction.

### 3. Initiate Cash-Out

Select an agent and initiate a USDC cash-out request.

### 4. Lock USDC in Escrow

The user's USDC is temporarily locked in a **Soroban escrow contract** while the cash exchange takes place.

### 5. Receive Local Cash

The agent hands over the agreed amount of local currency to the user.

### 6. Confirm & Release

Once the cash handover is confirmed, the escrow releases the USDC to the agent.

**Find Agent → Request Cash → Lock USDC → Receive Cash → Confirm → Release**

##  Why Soroban Escrow?

Trust is one of the biggest challenges in peer-to-peer cash exchanges.

AfricCash explores how a **Soroban smart contract** can act as an escrow layer between the user and agent.

Instead of immediately sending USDC to the agent:

**USDC → Escrow → Cash Handover → Confirmation → Agent**

This creates a programmable workflow for managing the digital side of the exchange.



##  Why Stellar?

AfricCash uses Stellar for the digital settlement layer because it provides infrastructure for fast, low-cost digital asset transfers.

The project explores how Stellar and Soroban can extend digital payments into **real-world local cash networks**.
